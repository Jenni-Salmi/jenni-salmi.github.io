About Me

I was born and raised in the countryside of Eastern Finland, nearly equidistant from the nearest bookstore and the Russian border. I wanted to write before I could read; one of my first written words was “Spede.” I adulted as an English literature major at the University of Joensuu, where I also taught Finnish as a Second Language and Academic English at the university’s Language Centre.

When I immigrated to the United States I put my language expertise to good use in a variety of fields, from education and literary publishing to video games and related hardware at Valve/Steam—and everything inbetween. As a life-long learner of my first language and a host of others to keep myself on my toes and curious about the world outside my own immediate sphere, I’m thrilled to act as a conduit between languages and cultures, ensuring that not just words and concepts are accurately translated, but that emotions are conveyed as well.

I also love visual communication and storytelling in the form of comics, zines, and sketch notes. Check out my Substack for drawings, comics, and other creative writing!
